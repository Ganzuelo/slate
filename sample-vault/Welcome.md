# Welcome to Slate

Your ideas deserve a place of their own.

This workspace keeps your notes as ordinary Markdown files. Start writing, connect an idea with [[Making connections]], or explore the graph in the left rail.

## A few small beginnings

- Write a thought you want to keep.
- Make a [[Projects/Reading list|reading list]].
- Open today's daily note from the calendar button.
- Switch to Preview to read without distraction.

## Yours, on disk

Press **Ctrl+S** to save. Changes are saved only when you choose; a dot-free status of “Saved to disk” confirms success.

There is no community plugin loader, cloud sync, or remote content in this prototype.
